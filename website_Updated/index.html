About me

I am currently working with the Landslides Team and EIS Team in Hydrological Research Laboratory of NASA Goddard Space Flight Center. My research is focused on mainstreaming multimission satellite observations in water resources monitoring and hydro-climatic disaster management. Passionate about application of AI, Cloud Computing, and Interactive and dynamic hydrologic application design and development.

More specific primary research interests are:

  • Satellite remote sensing application in water resources and hydro-climatic disaster management
  • Open source cloud computing and data science application in hydrology
  • Scientific visualization and analysis of geospatial big data
